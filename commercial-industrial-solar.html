Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial Solar FAQs

Clear, factual answers to common questions businesses ask before going solar.

Commercial solar systems typically reduce daytime electricity bills by 50% to 80%, depending on your power consumption profile, roof space, and local utility tariff slab. For most industrial and commercial entities in Tamil Nadu, this results in a full system payback within 3 to 4 years, followed by over two decades of virtually free daytime solar power.
System sizing is determined by analyzing your monthly units (kWh) consumed, sanctioned load (kVA/kW), daytime operating hours, and available shadow-free area. As a rule of thumb, a 10 kW system generates approximately 40 to 45 units daily, requiring roughly 800 to 1,000 sq. ft. of shadow-free rooftop space. SolarQube provides an exact sizing analysis during our site survey.
Yes. Solar can be mounted on metal trapezoidal/corrugated sheets, standing seam roofs, and concrete (RCC) terraces. For metal sheds, we utilize non-penetrating aluminum clamps and EPDM-sealed fasteners to eliminate roof piercing and prevent any water leakage. Our structural engineers verify load-bearing capacity before installation.
Modern high-efficiency Mono PERC / TOPCon solar panels require approximately 80 to 90 sq. ft. of shadow-free area per kilowatt (kW). For example, a 50 kW industrial rooftop system requires approximately 4,000 to 4,500 sq. ft. of usable roof space.
Under net-metering and gross-metering regulations in Tamil Nadu, any surplus solar electricity generated during low-consumption periods (e.g., Sundays or factory holidays) is automatically exported back to the grid. Your bi-monthly electricity bill reflects energy credits for the exported units.
Physical on-site installation typically takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on the system capacity (e.g., 20 kW to 150 kW). The overall timeline from initial design approval to DISCOM bi-directional net-meter commissioning usually spans 4 to 8 weeks, with SolarQube handling all statutory liaison.
Solar photovoltaic systems have zero moving parts and require minimal maintenance—primarily periodic water cleaning of module glass to remove dust and dirt, along with scheduled electrical safety checks. SolarQube offers comprehensive AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts) including thermal imaging and generation monitoring.
Yes. We provide complete turnkey liaison, managing TANGEDCO net-metering approvals, CEIG electrical inspectorate clearances (for systems requiring statutory safety certification), synchronization permissions, and grid connectivity documentation.
