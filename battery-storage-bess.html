Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)

Store energy. Use it when you need it.

Pair your solar system — or your existing grid connection — with battery storage so power is available on your terms: during outages, after sunset, or whenever tariffs and demand charges are highest.

The Basics

What is BESS?

A Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) is a bank of rechargeable batteries, paired with an inverter and smart controller, that stores electricity for later use. It can charge from your solar panels during the day, or from the grid during low-tariff hours, and release that stored power exactly when you need it — during a power cut, in the evening after the sun goes down, or at times when drawing from the grid is most expensive.

In short: it turns intermittent, time-bound power generation into power you can use on your own schedule.

The Combination

Solar + BESS

A solar system generates the most power in the middle of the day — often more than your home or business can use right then. Without storage, that extra energy is exported back to the grid, usually at a lower rate than what you pay to import power. Add a battery, and that excess daytime solar is stored on-site instead, ready to power your home or facility in the evening, overnight, or during a grid outage. The result: a higher share of your own solar generation is actually used by you, your electricity bill drops further, and you gain a reliable backup layer — all from the same rooftop system.
