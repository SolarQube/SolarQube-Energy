Founder Story Jalakandapuram, Salem

From Engineering to a Purpose in Renewable Energy

Surya Gnanavel, Founder — on site at a recent installation.

"Solar adoption shouldn't feel complicated. Our goal is to make clean energy a smarter, simpler, more dependable choice."

— Surya Gnanavel, Founder
B.E. Mechanical · PGD Renewable Energy (NPTI) · M.Sc. Renewable Energy Management (Coventry, UK)

SolarQube was founded by Surya Gnanavel, an engineer with a passion for renewable energy and a mission to make solar power more accessible, transparent, and practical for everyday customers.

His journey began with a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from Sona College of Technology, followed by advanced specialization with a Post Graduate Diploma in Renewable Energy and Smart Grid Technologies from NPTI Faridabad, and a Master of Science in Renewable Energy Management from Coventry University, UK.

Surya saw an opportunity to bring together technical rigor, grid expertise, and a customer-first approach. Today, SolarQube handles every step with clarity and precision — from understanding energy needs and engineering high-efficiency systems to hassle-free subsidy processing and dependable complete installation.
