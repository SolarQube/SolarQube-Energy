Solar in Tharamangalam: frequently asked questions

How much does solar cost in Tharamangalam? expand_more

The cost depends on system size, panel and inverter choice, roof type and structure, and whether subsidy applies. We do not publish a one-size-fits-all price. Please call or WhatsApp us, or fill the form on this page, and we will arrange a free site assessment and a written quotation for your requirement in Tharamangalam.

How does the PM Surya Ghar subsidy work? expand_more

PM Surya Ghar is a central scheme that supports residential rooftop solar. The subsidy depends on the capacity installed and the scheme rules at the time. We handle registration, documentation, installation and commissioning paperwork. Contact us for the current slab for your system size.

What is net metering and will it work for my Tharamangalam property? expand_more

Net metering uses a bi-directional meter that records the power you take from the grid and the power your solar plant sends back. The units you export are adjusted against your consumption, so you pay only for the difference. We apply for it with TANGEDCO on your behalf.

Do you install commercial and industrial solar in Tharamangalam? expand_more

Yes. We provide EPC and annual maintenance (AMC) for factories, mills, hospitals and commercial buildings, and can advise on accelerated depreciation benefits, which you should confirm with your chartered accountant.

Do you cover villages around Tharamangalam? expand_more

We serve Tharamangalam, the villages around it and the rest of Tamil Nadu. Send us your location and we will arrange a site survey.
