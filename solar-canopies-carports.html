What Makes Solar Canopies the Smartest Dual-Use Investment?

Solar canopies (or solar carports) are overhead steel structures that host high-efficiency photovoltaic panels while providing shaded, weather-protected vehicle parking bays below.

Unlike rooftop solar—which is constrained by roof structural load limits, skylights, or HVAC ducting—solar carports transform open tarmac into self-sufficient power plants with zero disruption to daily commercial workflows.

Engineering Typologies

Engineered Carport Structural Configurations

Every parking layout is unique. We fabricate custom hot-dip galvanized and high-tensile steel frames optimized for ease of vehicular ingress and maximum module density.

arrow_upward

Single Cantilever (L-Frame)

Designed for single-row parking along boundary walls and perimeters. Columns are placed at the rear to eliminate door-opening obstacles.

Bay Span: 1 Row (5m – 6m depth)
Best For: Perimeter lots & boundary bays
view_column

Dual Cantilever (T-Frame)

Center-column spine supporting two opposing rows of parked cars. Maximizes steel efficiency and reduces foundation footprint by 50%.

Bay Span: 2 Rows (10m – 12m depth)
Best For: Central parking aisles & corporate lots
table_rows

Clear-Span Truss Framework

High-clearance wide-span frameworks designed for commercial logistics, heavy truck yards, buses, and fleet transit terminals.

Clearance: 3.5m – 4.8m height
Best For: Industrial depots & bus transit bays
