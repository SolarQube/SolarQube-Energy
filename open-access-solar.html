Grid Delivery Mechanism

The Open Access Framework

Open Access is the regulatory framework (Electricity Act 2003 & Green Energy Open Access Rules) enabling HT consumers to procure electricity directly from independent renewable power producers using the state transmission (TANTRANSCO) and distribution (TANGEDCO) grid networks.

Commercial Contracting Model

Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)

A Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) is a bilateral, legally binding contract between the power consumer (you) and the power developer (SolarQube / IPP) establishing a fixed, discounted unit tariff (₹/kWh) for solar power supplied over an agreed term of 10 to 25 years.

  • check_circle Zero Capital Expenditure: The solar developer finances, builds, and operates the plant
  • check_circle Inflation-Proof Energy Rates: Protect your operations against frequent DISCOM tariff hikes
  • check_circle Pay-As-You-Consume: Pay strictly for the actual clean units generated and wheeled

Group Captive Model

26% Equity • 51% Consumption Rule

The power buyer holds a minimum 26% equity stake in the Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) power plant and consumes 51% of generated power. Crucial Benefit: 100% exemption from Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) and Additional Surcharge (AS).

Additional Surcharge (AS): EXEMPT (₹0.00)
Upfront Equity: Nominal (~5%-10% of plant cost)

Frequently Asked Questions

Open Access Solar & PPA FAQs

Key questions regarding regulatory approvals, banking, billing adjustments, and Group Captive rules.

Under the Ministry of Power's Green Energy Open Access (GEOA) Rules, the minimum sanctioned load requirement for procuring renewable energy has been reduced from 1 MW down to 100 kW. Commercial and industrial consumers connected at 11 kV, 22 kV, or 33 kV with a sanctioned demand of 100 kW or higher can now participate in green power wheeling.
In Tamil Nadu and across India, Section 42 of the Electricity Act 2003 grants Group Captive consumers a 100% statutory exemption from Cross-Subsidy Surcharge (CSS) and Additional Surcharge (AS). In third-party PPAs, CSS (which can be ₹1.50 to ₹2.50 per unit) is levied by the DISCOM, reducing your net savings. Group Captive structures preserve maximum tariff savings.
The solar power generated at the developer's plant is metered via Special Energy Meters (ABT/TOD compliant) and communicated to the SLDC. At the end of the billing cycle, TANGEDCO deducts the wheeled solar units from your HT meter consumption bill. You pay SolarQube/IPP for the solar units at the agreed lower PPA rate and pay TANGEDCO only for the remaining grid power and fixed demand charges.
Solar PPAs include structured Take-or-Pay or Minimum Guaranteed Off-take thresholds with banking provisions as permitted by state regulatory commissions. SolarQube sizes the allocation based on your base load (typically 60%–80% of minimum daytime consumption) so that you never over-procure power during low production periods.
If contracting power from an existing operational SolarQube solar park, Open Access regulatory approvals, SLDC clearances, and metering synchronization typically take 30 to 45 days. For new dedicated off-site park capacity, complete execution takes 4 to 6 months.
